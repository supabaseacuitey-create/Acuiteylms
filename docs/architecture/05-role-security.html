Strict Contextual Scoping

Financial Privacy
Payment transactions, UPI identifiers, and revenue reports are isolated exclusively to the Finance Administrator role.
Acuitey Role Customizations
Custom capabilities such as local/acuitey:override_routing are defined in db/access.php and assigned via standard Moodle roles.
